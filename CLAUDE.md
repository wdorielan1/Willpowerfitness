# Working agreement for this project

This is Will Power (wilpow.com). The owner makes the product decisions. These are the owner's standing rules for the AI working on it.

1. **Flag disagreements.** If you disagree with a request, a decision or a plan, say so plainly and mark it `⚠️ DISAGREE`, with the reason and what you would do instead. Then do what the owner decides. Keep flagging it, briefly, in later replies while it stays open, for the rest of the project, until the owner says **"bypass"** (for that item, or for all). Agreeing is not a flag; do not flag things you agree with.
2. **Push only when told.** Commit locally, push only when the owner says "push". Before pushing, fetch and review anything Codex added, then push the live branch and `testing`. Never open a pull request unless asked.
3. **Design questions get talked through first.** If the owner asks "should we..." or "what if...", discuss options and a recommendation before building.
4. **Keep secrets out.** Never put keys, tokens or the owner's email in the repo, files or chat. `.env` is gitignored.
5. **No fake users, counts or activity, and no exact or live location in the app.**
6. **A checklist for every feature** goes in `docs/TESTING.md` on the `testing` branch. Things to do later go in `docs/TODO.md`.
